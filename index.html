How do I start my property search with Gsquare?

Simply contact our team through our website or call us directly. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs, budget, and preferences, then provide you with a curated list of properties that match your criteria.

What areas do you specialize in?

We specialize in premium properties across major metropolitan areas including New York, California, Florida, and Texas. Our expertise covers luxury residential, commercial, and investment properties in prime locations.

Do you offer property management services?

Yes, we provide comprehensive property management services including tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance coordination, and financial reporting for investment properties.

What financing options are available?

We work with a network of trusted lenders to provide various financing options including conventional loans, jumbo loans, and investment property financing. Our team will help you find the best rates and terms.

The timeline varies depending on the property type and financing, but typically ranges from 30-60 days from offer acceptance to closing. We'll guide you through each step to ensure a smooth process.
